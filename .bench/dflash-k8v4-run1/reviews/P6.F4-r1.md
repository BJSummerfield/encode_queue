## Residual risks (carried from the worker report, confirmed acceptable)

- `pid_is_alive` is `/proc`-based (Linux-only, documented): on non-Linux hosts every lock reads stale and is always reclaimed — accepted per D2 for this single-user Linux-devbox tool.
- TOCTOU window between stale-removal and the retry `create_new` — bounded to 3 attempts, then the named `Io` error ("could not acquire the queue lock after 3 attempts"); the documented bounded trade-off of D2's design.
- The dead-PID probe assumes the machine's `pid_max` is far below 4,000,000,000 (same assumption D2 mandates for the unit tests; confirmed: probed PIDs 3,999,999,999 were dead here).
- A PID-recycled live process holding the same recorded PID is refused with `HeldBy` (safe direction); a `start` killed mid-drain leaves a lock that is only reclaimed once that PID is actually dead — inherent to the spec-mandated PID-in-file design.
